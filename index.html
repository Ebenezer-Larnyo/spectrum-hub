      How the list is made

      1. A crawler visits public pages from the sources in sources.json, following each site’s robots.txt rules.
      2. It reads each page’s title and summary and sorts it into the topics above.
      3. It removes duplicates and refreshes this page’s data, with the date each page was checked.

      Community space (planned)

      Research on autism discussions online found that people want to feel understood and to talk with each other and with health professionals. A text-first AI companion is now available under Companion. A moderated community of people needs accounts, safety rules and trained moderators, so it is not part of this version.

      About this information

      Spectrum Hub links to other sites and does not give medical advice. Talk to a qualified health professional about decisions for you or your family. Entries marked “starter entry” have not been crawled yet.

      Talk it through

      A friendly AI companion for autistic people and caregivers. It listens, talks things over, and points you to resources in the Hub. It is not a person, a therapist or a doctor, and you can take as long as you like to reply.

      Need urgent help? In the US, call or text 988, or call 911 in an emergency. Elsewhere, contact your local emergency number.

      Memory (optional)

      Off by default. If you turn it on, only your preferences are kept on this device, never what we talk about. Your messages are sent to Claude to write replies and are not stored by this page. Voice input uses your browser’s speech service, which may send audio to your browser’s provider.
