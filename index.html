      What the companion remembers

        About Spectrum Hub

        Spectrum Hub collects and sorts links from selected public autism sources into one calm, searchable place, for autistic people, caregivers and professionals.

        Why it exists

        A 2024 study of autism discussions on Reddit, led by Dr. Ebenezer Larnyo (Larnyo, Nutakor, Addai-Dansoh and Nkrumah, Frontiers in Psychiatry 15:1441349), found that people want reliable health information, support for diagnosis and caregiving, and a community that helps them feel understood. It recommended accessible digital platforms that bring this together. This site is a prototype of that idea. Read the article (opens in a new tab).

        How the list is made

        A program called SpectrumHubBot visits a short list of public pages from government health agencies, universities and nonprofits. It reads each page’s title and summary, sorts it into topics, removes duplicates and records the date it was checked. A person chooses which sources it may visit. Labels such as “Government” or “Nonprofit” say who runs a source. They are not a guarantee that the information is right for you.

        What it is not

        It does not give medical advice, diagnose anyone or replace a health professional. It does not independently verify, review or endorse the sites it links to, and their content is not clinically validated. The AI companion is a computer program, not a person, therapist or doctor.

        Get in touch

        Questions, corrections, suggestions for sources, or a request to stop SpectrumHubBot visiting your site: spectrumhub.project@protonmail.com. Autistic people and caregivers who can tell us what would make this easier to use are especially welcome.

        Privacy and safety

        Last updated 27 October 2026. Short version: no accounts, no tracking tools, and nothing saved about you unless you choose it.

        If you need urgent help

        This site is not an emergency service. In the US, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or call 911. Elsewhere, contact your local emergency number.

        What this site does not do

        It has no accounts and no advertising, and it does not add analytics or tracking tools. It does not sell data.

        What is stored on your device

        Nothing, unless you turn on “Remember my preferences” in the Companion page. Then only your preferences are kept in your browser, such as text size, colors and your preferred name. Conversations are never saved. You can see each item, forget one, forget all, or turn Memory off at any time.

        Other companies involved

        • Hosting. The page is served by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors’ addresses under GitHub’s own privacy statement.
        • Fonts. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your address when the page loads.
        • Linked sites. Resources open on other websites that have their own privacy practices.

        The AI companion

        When you send a message, the text is sent to a server run for this project and then to Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, to write a reply. This page does not store your messages, and the project’s server is set up not to log them. Anthropic processes them under its own privacy policy. Please do not share anything you would not want an AI service to process, such as full names, addresses or medical record details. If you use voice input, your browser’s speech service may send your audio to its provider. Messages that mention self-harm, abuse or immediate danger get a fixed safety message and are not sent to the AI.

        The companion is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please do not use it without a trusted adult. It can make mistakes, so check anything important with a qualified professional.

        The crawler

        SpectrumHubBot reads only public pages, follows each site’s robots.txt rules, waits between requests, does not visit forums or social media, and collects no personal data. Site owners can ask us to stop at the email above.
